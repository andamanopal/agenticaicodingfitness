Calibrate the wrist camera

Print the 8 × 6 inner-corner checkerboard at 100% scale. Show it from at least ten varied angles; keep the full pattern sharp and visible.

Open printable target
0 of 10 samples captured
Use the live cameraMove either the arm or target between samples. Repeated copies of the same view do not constrain focal length and distortion well.

Teach one safe travel pose and three camera poses

Place the checkerboard face-up and flat. Measure the first inner corner nearest the red marker from the tabletop point directly below the fixed-base center: +X goes into the desk and +Y goes robot-left. Board yaw is the counter-clockwise angle from robot +X to printed +X. The portal accounts for printed +Y running down the page and printed +Z pointing into the table. Keep the board fixed for all three recordings.

Accuracy-criticalThese measurements register camera pixels to robot coordinates. A 20 mm origin error becomes roughly a 20 mm grasp error. Teach genuinely different left/center/right positions with at least 15° of wrist-frame rotation across the set.
